01THE STORY

A sound that
changed everything.

Born in Gary, Indiana, in 1958, Michael Jackson found his voice early. As the youngest lead singer of the Jackson 5, he grew from a gifted child performer into a defining force in popular music.

His solo journey brought a new scale of ambition. Off the Wall revealed his musical range; Thriller became a global phenomenon and helped reshape the relationship between music and visual storytelling.

His performances were built on precision, imagination and movement. The moonwalk, the sharp silhouettes and the unmistakable stage presence became part of a visual language recognised around the world.

Michael Jackson's influence continues through the artists, dancers and audiences who discover his work today. His story remains part of the history of modern entertainment.
